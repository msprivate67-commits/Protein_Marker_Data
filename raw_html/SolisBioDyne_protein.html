                cDNA synthesis

                NEW!
                FIREScript® FLEX H(-) KIT

                FIREScript® FLEX H(-) Reverse Transcriptase (RT) is an in silico engineered RNA-directed DNA polymerase designed for high-sensitivity cDNA synthesis and robust performance under challenging conditions.

                NEW!
                FIREScript® ULTRA H(+) KIT

                FIREScript® ULTRA H(+) Reverse Transcriptase (RT) is an in silico engineered RNA-directed DNA polymerase designed for fast, efficient, and reproducible cDNA synthesis.

                RiboGrip®️ Glycerol-Free RNase Inhibitor (220 U/µl)

                RiboGrip® RNase Inhibitor is an in silico-designed protein-based ribonuclease inhibitor with enhanced stability. Unique high concentration and glycerol free formulation is ideal for lyophilization.

                RiboGrip® RNase Inhibitor (220U/µl)

                RiboGrip® RNase Inhibitor (220 U/µl) is an in silico-designed protein-based ribonuclease inhibitor with enhanced stability. Effective protection against RNase A, RNase B and RNase C.

                FIREScript® KIT

                FIREScript® KIT contains a powerful genetically modified MMLV-based FIREScript® reverse transcriptase with increased thermostability and exceptional stability at room temperature. The kit format contains only the enzyme and reaction buffer allowing you to choose other components according to your preference.

                FIREScript® RT cDNA synthesis KIT

                A flexible and robust cDNA synthesis kit contains all the reagents you need for first-strand cDNA synthesis.  Individual components enable thorough assay optimization.

                FIREScript® RT cDNA synthesis MIX

                All-inclusive and the most convenient product format for first-strand cDNA synthesis. A combined enzyme mix (FIREScript® and RiboGrip®) and a reaction premix in separate tubes allowing you to choose the most appropriate priming option.

                SOLIScript® KIT

                SOLIScript® KIT contains a unique in silico-designed  SOLIScript® reverse transcriptase with high thermostability and exceptional stability at room temperature. SOLIScript® suits best with oligo dT and gene-specific primers. The kit format contains only the enzyme and reaction buffer allowing you to choose other components according to your preference.

                SOLIScript® RT cDNA synthesis KIT

                A flexible and highly specific cDNA synthesis kit that contains all the reagents you need for first-strand cDNA synthesis. Individual components enable thorough assay optimization. 

                SOLIScript® RT cDNA synthesis MIX

                All-inclusive and the most convenient product format for first-strand cDNA synthesis using gene-specific primers. A combined enzyme mix (SOLIScript® and RiboGrip®) and a reaction premix in separate tubes. Primers not included.
