RELAYTR FRET Platform

Fluorescence Resonance Energy Transfer (FRET) is a powerful technique used to detect molecular proximity between a donor and an acceptor fluorophore within a distance of 1–10 nm. In time-resolved FRET (TR-FRET) assays, the long fluorescence lifetime of lanthanides (such as terbium and europium) enables delayed signal detection, effectively eliminating short-lived background fluorescence. This time-resolved measurement significantly enhances assay sensitivity and specificity. 

Our RELAYTR FRET Platform leverages terbium and europium chelate-based donors paired with fluorescein- and Cy5-based acceptors. These fluorophores can be covalently conjugated to targets via free thiol or amine groups, ensuring robust and reliable performance and versative assay formats for detecting molecular interactions, post-translational modifications, enzyme activity, protein abundance, and more.

We provide both ready-to-use reagents and customized CRO services to support diverse research and development needs.
