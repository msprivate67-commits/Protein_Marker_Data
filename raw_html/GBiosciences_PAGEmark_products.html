PAGEmark™ Blue PLUS Protein Markers
A blue protein standard with 12 prestained proteins covering a wide range of molecular weights: 10-240kDa in Tris-Glycine buffer and 9-235kDa in Bis-Tris (MOPS) buffer or Bis-Tris (MES) buffer.
 
Proteins are covalently coupled with a blue chromophore and two reference bands, 25kDa and 72kDa, are enhanced in intensity for easier reference.
 
PAGEmark™ Tricolor PLUS Protein Markers
A ready-to-use three-color protein standard with 13 prestained proteins covering a wide range of molecular weights: 5-245kDa in Tris-Glycine buffer or 3.5-235kDa in Bis-Tris (MOPS) buffer or Bis-Tris (MES) buffer). 
 
Proteins are covalently coupled with a blue chromophore except for two reference bands. A green chromophore at 25kDa and a red chromophore at 75kDa. 
 
The PAGEmark™ line do not require boiling or denaturing, simply load on gel, and are readily transferred to Western blot membranes (PVDF and nitrocellulose).

Features
  • Ready-to-use
  • Blue or tri-colored pre-stained markers

Applications

  • Prestained protein ladder for molecular weight determination by  protein electrophoresis
  • Wide range protein markers
