RNase 4
RNase 4 是一种单链RNA 内切酶，它在尿苷-嘌呤序列处切割 RNA。它提供有针对性的切割并耐受各种尿苷修饰。与其他RNase相比，它产生更多的异质3'末端寡核苷酸。

High-quality and pure RNA is a requirement for RNA therapeutics to avoid unwanted immune responses, not just a “nice to have.” In vitro transcription can be optimized, but additional cleanup may be needed. Enzymatic removal of dsRNA using RNase III preserves ssRNA integrity when refined using a protocol developed by NEB scientists.
