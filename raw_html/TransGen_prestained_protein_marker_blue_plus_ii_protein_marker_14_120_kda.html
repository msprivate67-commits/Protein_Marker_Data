    Product Details

    Blue Plus® II Protein Marker is composed of eight prestained proteins ranging from 14 to 120 kDa. The proteins of 50 kDa and 120 kDa bands are covalently coupled to orange dye. The protein of 14 kDa band is covalently coupled to yellow dye. The other five bands are covalently coupled to blue dye. This prestained protein marker is designed for monitoring the progress of SDS-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis, for assessing transfer efficiency onto PVDF or NC membranes. Clear colored bands can be visible on PVDF or NC membranes. The orange and bright yellow bands make it easy to determine the direction of the transfer.
