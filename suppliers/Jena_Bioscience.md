# Jena Bioscience
**Official site:** jenabioscience.com
**Protein markers found:** 4

Jena Bioscience (Jena, Germany) offers a single line of **Prestained Protein
Markers** under the "Blue*" naming scheme, all in the
`/molecular-biology/enzymes-protein-markers/protein-markers` category.

Category page: https://www.jenabioscience.com/molecular-biology/enzymes-protein-markers/protein-markers

All four are ready-to-use, supplied as **2 × 250 µL** in loading buffer, shipped
on gel packs, stored at −20 °C, shelf life 12 months, purity >95 % (SDS-PAGE),
recommended load 3–5 µL/lane. The three-color variants have two reference bands
(green at 25 kDa, red at 75 kDa); the rest are blue.
