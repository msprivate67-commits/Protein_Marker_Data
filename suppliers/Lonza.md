# Lonza
**Official site:** lonza.com (shop: bioscience.lonza.com)
**Protein markers found:** 1 (current) + 2 legacy (documented)

Lonza's protein-electrophoresis marker line is the **ProSieve** brand. Only one
protein marker is currently orderable on the Lonza bioscience shop; the older
ProSieve Color Protein Marker and the unstained ProSieve Protein Marker are
discontinued but their instruction sheets are still hosted.

> Note: `lonza.com` itself returns **HTTP 403 (Akamai)** to bots. All data below
> was retrieved from the shop subdomain `bioscience.lonza.com`, which geo-
> redirects to `/lonza_bs/CN/en/...` from this environment.

blots via S-protein AP/HRP conjugate. No longer found as an orderable product in

- Catalog #: ~**50540** family (no live product page)
- Type: Unstained, S-Tagged (western-blot detectable)
- Bands: **10 bands**
- Sizes (kDa): **5, 10, 15, 25, 35, 50, 75, 100, 150, 225 kDa**
  (50 kDa band at double intensity)
