# Carl Roth
**Official site:** carlroth.com
**Protein markers found:** 5

Carl Roth (Karlsruhe, Germany) distributes a line of **prestained protein
ladders** under its own "ROTH" brand in the category
`/protein-markers-prestained/`. These are manufactured by **Bio-Helix** (Taiwan)
— the same BlueRay / BlueEye / BlueElf products are also sold by Jena Bioscience
and other distributors. All are ready-to-use, 500 µL, for SDS-PAGE + western-
blot transfer monitoring.

## Notes
- **OEM relationship**: The BlueRay/BlueEye/BlueElf products are Bio-Helix
  (Taiwan) manufactured and badged for Carl Roth. The same physical products are
  sold by Jena Bioscience (PS-103/104/105) and others. There are minor label
  differences (Roth quotes BlueElf as 3.5–245 kDa; Jena quotes 5–245 kDa).
- Storage: −20 °C long-term; 4 °C up to 3 months; 25 °C up to 2 weeks.
- Buffer (BlueRay/Eye/Elf/Aqua): 20 mM Tris-phosphate pH 7.5, 2% SDS, 0.2 mM
  DTT, 3.6 M urea, 15% glycerol. PiNK Plus uses 1 mM DTT, 4.8 M urea, 12%
  glycerol.
- Roth does NOT sell an unstained protein marker — all 5 are prestained.
- German SDS (SDB-1GGx-DE-DE.pdf) are also available on each product page but
  not downloaded this session (only English specifications were saved).
