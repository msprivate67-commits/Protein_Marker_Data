# Phage and Plasmid Markers (Fermentas / Thermo Fisher classic DNA markers)
**Official site:** fermentas.com → thermofisher.com (Fermentas brand now under Thermo Fisher)
**Protein markers found:** 0 (in this product family)

## Conclusion
The "Phage and Plasmid Markers" entry refers to the classic **Fermentas collection of DNA molecular-weight markers**, all based on bacteriophage / plasmid DNA digests:
- **λ DNA / HindIII** (lambda phage DNA digest) — large DNA fragment sizing
- **ΦX174 DNA / HaeIII** (phiX174 phage DNA digest) — small DNA fragment sizing
- **pUC19 DNA / MspI** (plasmid DNA digest) — DNA sizing
- **GeneRuler™** DNA ladders (1 kb, 100 bp, 50 bp, etc.)

These are **DNA-only** products. **No protein marker / protein ladder** belongs to this phage-and-plasmid marker family.

## Note on protein markers
Fermentas/Thermo Fisher *does* manufacture protein markers (e.g. **PageRuler™, PageRuler Plus, Spectra™, Novex® Sharp** prestained ladders), but those are a **separate product line** under Thermo Fisher (covered by the Thermo Fisher supplier file), **not** part of the "phage and plasmid markers" DNA-marker family named here. So this specific entry contributes **0 independent protein products.**
