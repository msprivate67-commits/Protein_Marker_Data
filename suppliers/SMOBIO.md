# SMOBIO Technology, Inc.
**Official site:** www.smobio.com
**Protein markers found:** 14

SMOBIO (Taiwan) produces the ExcelBand™ and ProMetric™ lines of protein markers, all pre-stained. The category page is at https://www.smobio.com/shop/category/protein-markers-10 . All products ship ready-to-use in 2x loading buffer and are stored at -20 °C. Band molecular weights below are the Tris-Glycine gel values (the most common reference buffer); in Bis-Tris MOPS/MES gels the apparent MW shifts down slightly (e.g. 245→235, 180→170, 140→130, 100→93, 72→68).
